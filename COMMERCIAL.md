## When you need a commercial license

You need a commercial license to use SlintPlot for a commercial purpose in
software that is not distributed under the GPLv3, for example:

- in a closed-source product, app or device you sell, license or ship to
  customers
- in software or services your company uses to run its business
- in client work or consulting

## How to get one

Commercial licenses are available from the copyright holder. To ask for one,
email <license@slintplot.dev> and say briefly how you plan to use the
library.

## Slint's own license

SlintPlot is built on [Slint](https://slint.dev), which has its own
licensing (GPLv3, a royalty-free license for desktop and web apps, or a paid
license). Your application needs a suitable Slint license in addition to a
SlintPlot license.
